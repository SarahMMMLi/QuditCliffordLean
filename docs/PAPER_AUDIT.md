## Consistent groups for the selected convention

Let `omega = exp(2*pi*i/d)`, `sigma = -omega`, with `d` an odd prime.

- `sigma` has order `2*d`; `sigma^d=-1` and `sigma^(d+1)=omega`.
- Let `Cfig_n` be the subgroup generated by `sigma,H,S,CZ` under composition and tensor product, as Figure 1 specifies.
- Let `Pfig_n` be the subgroup generated by `sigma,X,Z`; its scalar subgroup is `mu_(2d) = { +/-omega^t }`.
- The exact scalar subgroup of `Cfig_n` is `mu_(2d)`. The paper's ring-membership and root-of-unity argument gives the upper bound; the generator sigma gives the converse. The sign-exclusion step of Proposition 2.17 applies only to the older omega-only group and is omitted here.
- The quotient theorems `Cfig_n/Pfig_n ≃ Sp(2n,ZMod d)` and `Cfig_n/mu_(2d) ≃ PhaseSpace ⋊ Sp` are now proved in `ExactNormalForm.lean` and `CliffordSemidirect.lean`. The group is independently defined as the subgroup closure of actual primitive unitary matrices, not by these quotient descriptions.
- Under the selected convention the proved cardinality is `2*d^(2*n+1)*|Sp(2n,ZMod d)|`, and `|Pfig_n|=2*d^(2*n+1)`. The Clifford formula follows from a bijection with concrete exact normal forms, independently of the paper's omega-only group comparison.
- Dividing `Cfig_n` by the OLD omega-only Pauli group would leave an extra sign class, and would not give the claimed symplectic group.

Under this convention raw multipliers and exact SWAP belong to `Cfig`; it is unnecessary to replace them with signed lifts. Remark 2.15 remains useful for explaining why the older group differs.

### Derived gates and scalar details

Figure 2 (p. 4), Figure 4 (p. 15), and Appendix A.3 use the following exact matrix words:

- `Z = H^2*S*H^2*S^(-1)`.
- `X = H*S*H^2*S^(-1)*H`.
- `M_a = Legendre(a,d)*omega^((-a^2+4*a-2)/(8*a)) * Z^((1-a)/(2*a))*X^((1-a)/2)*S^(a^(-1))*H*S^a*H*S^(a^(-1))*H`.

Treating them as unrelated primitive matrices does not establish completeness for the displayed presentation.

## Repairs required in the surrounding text

4. **Separate rewrite congruences:** Definition 4.7 and Proposition 4.8 (pp. 25-26) use phase-discarded Pauli identities. An exact-sound Figure 1 congruence cannot literally derive `Z;X=X;Z`, because `ZX=omega*XZ`. Their intended proof belongs to a separate projective relation obtained by forgetting scalar corrections. Likewise their identity `id=H;S;H;S;H;S` is projective: T1 at `a=1` gives `(S*H)^3=omega^(-1/8)*I` exactly. At `d=3` the latter scalar is omega. These are proof-convention errors, not unsound Figure 1 rules.
5. **Exact lifting is not automatic identification:** Theorem 4.10's last paragraph (pp. 27-28) invokes the central-extension argument without enumerating its phase corrections or showing that the corrected rules are derived from Figure 1. 6. **Normal-form induction:** In the proof of Proposition 3.8 (Appendix E pp. 69-70), the adjusted automorphism fixes the last X,Z only up to phase, while equation (111) asserts exact factorization `phi'=phi'' tensor I`. Work on projective Pauli phase space, or remove the residual phases before asserting exact factorization. The project's phase-space proof should state this repair explicitly.
